The 2026 annual conference of the HUN-REN Data Repository Platform (ARP), held at the Humán Tudományok Kutatóháza in Budapest and streamed online. The programme examines the long-term sustainability, security, and innovation of research data repositories and data stewardship, from domestic good practice to the EOSC and OpenAIRE ecosystems.

András Vereckei presents in the afternoon session "Technikai fejlesztések és lehetőségek" on how large language models and the tools built on them are transforming research and the deposit of research data.

**When** October 8, 2026, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM CEST  
**Where** Humán Tudományok Kutatóháza, Tóth Kálmán utca 4, 1097 Budapest (and online)
